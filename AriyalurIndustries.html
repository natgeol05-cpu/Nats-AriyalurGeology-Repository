Industries in Ariyalur

The major industries relevant to Geologists are mainly Limestone Quarries and Cement Factories.

Limestone Quarries in Tamil Nadu

Limestone quarries are present in several districts in Tamil Nadu, with notable reserves in Salem, Tiruchirappalli, Ariyalur, Perambalur, and Thoothukudi. Fossiliferous limestone is found in Tiruchirappalli, Ariyalur, Perambalur, and Thoothukudi districts.

The total reserve of fossiliferous limestone is estimated at 670 million tonnes.

Limestone Quarries in Ariyalur

  • Kallankurichi Limestone Kankar Quarry: Public consultation held for expansion.
  • Pudupalayam village: Opposition raised over environmental impacts.
  • Ramco Cements: Limestone mining using conventional and non-conventional methods.
  • Alathiyur block: Part of Sendurai Taluk limestone belt under exploration.
  • Periakurichi Block: Preliminary exploration proposal submitted to NMET.
  • Periapurichi area: Dalmia Cement holds a mining lease; sedimentary limestone deposit.
  • Ariyalur and Sendurai Taluks: Presence of cement-grade limestone.

Data from public domain. For up-to-date details, refer to official online sources.

Cement Production in Tamil Nadu (Company-wise)

Major producers include Tamil Nadu Cements Corporation Ltd, Zuari Cement Ltd, UltraTech Cement Ltd, Dhandapani Cements (P) Ltd, The India Cements Ltd, and others. These companies operate multiple plants across the state, including Ariyalur and nearby regions.

Major Cement Producers

  • Tamil Nadu Cements Corporation Ltd (TANCEM): Ariyalur and Alangulam.
  • Zuari Cement Ltd: Athipattu plant (PPC and OPC).
  • UltraTech Cement Ltd: Reddipalayam and Arakkonam plants.
  • Dhandapani Cements (P) Ltd: Thathamangalam plant.
  • The India Cements Ltd: Narnamalpuram plant.
  • Chettinad Cement: Ariyalur, Karikkali, and Puliyur plants.

Data from public domain. For up-to-date details, refer to official online sources.

Cement Manufacturing Process

Cement manufacturing is a multi-step process: raw materials (limestone and clay) are mined, crushed, mixed, and heated in a kiln to form clinker. The clinker is then finely ground and blended with gypsum to produce cement.

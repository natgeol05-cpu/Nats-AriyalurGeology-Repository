Rastellum - bivalve

Description

Name: Rastellum
Species: pectinata
Formation: Kallankurichchi Formation
Locality: Ottakkoil
Classification: Mollusca - Bivalvia -Ostreida - Arctostreidae
Age: Range: from 161.2 to 66.043 million years ago

Fossils of Rastellum are found from the Middle Jurassic until the Late Cretaceous
(age range: from 161.2 to 66.043 million years ago.).
They are present in the Cretaceous marine strata throughout the world,
including in Ariyalur at Ottakkoil.

The shells of molluscs of Rastellum species can reach a length of about 10 cm (3.9 in).
These oysters had a narrow-valved, tubular shell, which was composed of regularly
alternating sharp ridges and deep folds.
The vulnerable commissure (along which the valves meet) was sunk.

Lifestyle: This genus inhabited warm waters, where it cemented itself
with the pointed vertebrae to shells or corals.
Oysters belonging to the genus Rastellum were stationary epifaunal suspension feeders.

Geology of Ariyalur

Overview of formations, fossils, industries, and educational resources

About Ariyalur Basin

Ariyalur is one of Tamil Nadu’s key geological regions, known for Cretaceous sedimentary formations, fossil-bearing beds, limestone resources, and educational importance for earth science learners.

This page provides access to major geology topics, fossil records, industrial geology references, and quick links to related sections of the website.

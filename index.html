Special Note

Special Note

Developed this Website for not only college students, but also for others. There are many who are fossil collectors and they are interested to know more about their fossils. We start and initiate you to identify Cretaceous vertebrate and invertebrate fossils from South India by experts available in this field.

We request you to send clear photographs of front, right side, left side and top view with a measuring scale, or a coin or a pen or a common item for visualising the relative size of fossil. Images in square frame (1:1 ratio) are preferred (if possible) as they occupy less space. Every specimen should have a field number visible on it for future reference. GPS data of collection site is desirable if available. If a query on such uploaded fossils is received, we will send a reply to your email ID within a reasonable period (say, within two weeks).
